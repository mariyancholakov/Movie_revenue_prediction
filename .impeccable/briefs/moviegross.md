# Surface brief — MOVIEGROSS (all surfaces)

Scope: whole-app replacement visual world. Visitor mode: **Operate** (browse + read predictions), with a **Persuade** masthead moment on the home surface (this is a showcase for the ML model). Redesign: preserves all product truth in PRODUCT.md; replaces the "cinema marquee at night" look.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A published **box-office ledger** — the model's predictions typeset like real weekend grosses in a trade paper, and its track record settled like an accounting book ("in the black / in the red"). It refuses the category default of glowing neon-on-dark movie UI and the generic hero-metric card grid; the numbers ARE the design, set in print.

OWN-WORLD: Ink on warm newsprint. Ground `#EDE7DA` paper, `#1C1814` printing ink, warm grays for metadata, ONE spot red `#C0281F` reserved for negatives/over-budget/misses and a few nameplate rules — nothing else is colored. Structure is carried by ruled hairlines, newspaper double-rules (thick+thin), and column rules, never by cards, glows, or shadows. Type: **Bodoni Moda** (trade-paper Didone masthead + headlines), **Libre Franklin** (Franklin-Gothic newspaper text/UI), **Courier Prime** (screenplay + accounting ledger figures — all money/data, tabular). No decorative eyebrow/kicker labels.

STORY: The visitor sees a credible box-office paper, browses films now in theaters, opens one, "runs the numbers," and reads a printed prediction (opening, predicted total, decay λ, est. profit) plus a decay chart. They trust it because the Finished ledger settles past predictions against reality in black and red.

FIRST VIEWPORT (home): Full-bleed nameplate "MOVIEGROSS" in heavy Bodoni, under a newspaper double-rule with a folio line (edition · date · "THE BOX-OFFICE ORACLE") and the model disclaimer as the imprint. Below: a one-line dek, then the "NOW IN THEATERS" picture-page grid of posters with printed captions. No glow, no bulbs, no gradient wash.

FORM: Trade-paper / accounting ledger (pinned by the user from a 3-option round; ranked #1 of the presented set). Code-led build (engine/comp tooling unavailable in this environment). Signature interaction: the predicted total **totes up** like an adding machine when a prediction lands; a quiet ink-settle draws the nameplate/rules on load; chart data points reveal a thin crosshair + Courier readout on hover. Reduced-motion safe (all default states visible).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
